About Turbograph v3.0.0

A drafting table for the velocity triangles of steam-turbine stages — De Laval, Curtis, Rateau and Parsons — plus a free-form stage for anything in between.

Nomenclature follows B.K. Venkanna's Fundamentals of Turbomachinery: U (blade speed), V/Vr (absolute / relative velocity), Vw/Vf (whirl / flow components), α/β (absolute / relative angles to the tangential direction).

Colour key: brass = U, blue = absolute velocity (V), red = relative velocity (Vr), teal = whirl component (Vw), violet = flow component (Vf).

Three modes, top centre: Free draw — place any vector by dragging or typing exact length & angle; Tutorial — a step-by-step build for the selected turbine; Construct — hand over U, the known angles and vectors, and the whole diagram is drawn and solved for you.

Snap (left rail) locks wherever you release a drag onto the nearest grid intersection, or magnetically onto any existing vector's tail or tip when you release within reach of one — so triangles close up exactly, tip to tail. Diagrams built by Tutorial or Construct are locked to protect the solved geometry; select one and choose Unlock to edit if you want to nudge it by hand.

Flip orientation (left rail, chevron icon) mirrors the whole diagram vertically — triangles pointing up from the baseline vs. down, matching whichever way your textbook or lecturer draws it. It's purely visual: labels, lengths, angles and every Results figure stay exactly as computed.

Results (right panel) update live from whatever's actually on the sheet, in Free draw as much as Construct.
